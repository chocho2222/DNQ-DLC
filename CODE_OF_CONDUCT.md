# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Project participants must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive
technical disagreement, credit prior work, protect private information and
avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, sexualized language,
deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and
misrepresenting experimental evidence or authorship.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the verified email
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove content,
reject contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently exclude a
participant. Reports will be handled confidentially and conflicts of interest
will be disclosed or delegated where possible.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, code review and
other spaces where a participant represents the project.
